## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to discuss **[brief description — e.g. "a potential
supplier relationship for resale inventory"]** (the "Purpose") and may
disclose confidential information to each other in the course of that

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed
by either Party, in any form, that is either marked confidential or
would reasonably be understood as confidential given its nature —
including pricing, supplier/customer lists, sourcing methods, deal
terms, financial information, and business plans.

Confidential Information does **not** include information that:
(a) was already known to the receiving Party before disclosure,
(b) becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party,
(c) is independently developed without using the disclosing Party's
information, or
(d) is rightfully received from a third party without a duty of
confidentiality.

## 3. Obligations

Each Party agrees to:
- use the other's Confidential Information only for the Purpose,
- protect it with at least the same care it uses for its own
  confidential information (and no less than reasonable care),
- not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent,
  except to employees/advisors who need it for the Purpose and are
  bound by confidentiality obligations at least as strict as this one,
  and
- return or destroy the other's Confidential Information upon request.

## 4. Term

This Agreement's confidentiality obligations remain in effect for
**[e.g. two (2) years]** from the date above, regardless of whether the
Parties proceed with any further relationship.

## 5. No License, No Obligation to Proceed

Nothing here grants either Party any license or rights to the other's
Confidential Information beyond what's needed for the Purpose. Nothing
here obligates either Party to enter into any further business
relationship.
