## 2. Independent Contractor Status

Contractor is an independent contractor, not an employee, of the
Company. Contractor is responsible for their own taxes (including
self-employment tax), insurance, and equipment unless otherwise agreed
in writing. This Agreement does not create a partnership, joint
venture, or employment relationship.

- Rate: **[$X per hour / per item / flat fee of $X for the project]**
- Payment schedule: **[e.g. "within 14 days of invoice"]**
- Method: **[e.g. "bank transfer, Stripe, check"]**

Company will request a completed Form W-9 from Contractor before the
first payment (see `W9_Request_Letter.md`) and will issue a Form 1099-NEC
if total payments in a calendar year meet the IRS reporting threshold.

## 4. Ownership of Work Product

Work product created specifically for the Company under this Agreement
(photos, listing copy, etc.) belongs to the Company upon full payment.
Contractor retains no ownership claim over delivered work product except
as separately agreed in writing (e.g. a portfolio-use right).

## 5. Confidentiality

Contractor agrees not to disclose the Company's confidential business
information (pricing, supplier relationships, customer information,
sourcing methods) to any third party, during or after this engagement.

## 6. Term and Termination

This Agreement begins on the date above and continues until the
Services are complete, or either Party terminates with **[e.g. "7 days'"]**
written notice. Company will pay for Services completed in good faith
up to the termination date.

## 7. No Exclusivity

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, Contractor is free to work for
other clients, and Company is free to engage other contractors.

regard to conflict-of-laws principles.
