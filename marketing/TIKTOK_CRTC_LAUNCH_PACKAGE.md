# CRTC TikTok Launch Package

## Core promise
**Don't search harder. Let CRTC find what everyone else missed.**

CRTC = Cooper River Trading Co. Opportunity Intelligence.
It hunts for mispriced, misidentified, badly titled, poorly photographed, overlooked, ending-soon, liquidation, and surplus opportunities — then estimates value and tells the reseller what to pay.

## Posting rules
- Lead with the mistake or opportunity in the first 1–2 seconds.
- Show the actual listing/screenshot whenever permission and platform rules allow.
- Never invent a profit claim. Label estimates as estimates.
- Blur personal information.
- End with one CTA: **"Try CRTC."**
- Favor 20–35 second videos.
- Use captions on every video.

## POST 05 — GOVERNMENT LOT
**Hook:** "Would you bid on a government computer lot without checking this first?"

**Script:**
"For computers, CRTC isn't satisfied with 'lot of laptops.' It wants the model, CPU generation, RAM, storage, locks, missing chargers, testing status, repair risk, fees, shipping and realistic resale value. Then it works backward to a maximum bid. That's the difference between buying a pallet and buying inventory."

**CTA:** "Know your max bid before you bid."
